Why lemonade?

  1. I originally called this project learnmode and then realized it had all the letters for lemonade!
  2. A play off of the age-old adage, "When life gives you lemons, make lemonade." Unrestricted access to AI has made it easy to echo but hard to learn. I'm adding friction back to my learning by auto-blocking major AIs and requiring myself to log intent in order to unblock.
